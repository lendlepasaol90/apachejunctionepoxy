Why epoxy works so well out here

It takes the heat

A properly installed system won't lift or soften when your garage hits triple digits, and the right topcoat won't release under hot tires.

Dust stays on top

A sealed floor stops concrete dusting and makes desert grit and monsoon mud easy to sweep or hose off.

It brightens the room

A glossy, light-colored floor reflects light, so garages and shops feel cleaner and need less lighting.

Spills wipe up

Oil, coolant, pool chemicals and fertilizer sit on the surface instead of soaking into the slab.

Questions we get a lot

How long does an epoxy floor last?

A residential garage floor with proper prep and a quality topcoat commonly lasts 10–20 years. Commercial floors depend on traffic; topcoats can be refreshed without starting over.

How long until I can park on it?

With a polyaspartic topcoat, you can usually walk on it the same evening and drive on it within 24–48 hours. Full epoxy systems need a few days more.

Will it get slippery?

Any glossy floor is slicker when wet. Flake broadcasts add texture, and we can add an anti-slip additive to the topcoat for garages, patios and pool decks.

Can you coat over my old coating or a cracked slab?

Usually yes. Peeling or failing coatings get ground off, and cracks and pits get repaired before anything new goes down. We'll tell you at the estimate what your slab needs.

Will it yellow in the sun?

Standard epoxy can amber under UV. That's why we finish garages and outdoor areas with a UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on square footage, slab condition and the system you choose. Estimates are free, and you'll get a written quote after we see the floor.
